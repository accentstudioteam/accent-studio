Rules

  • Clear space: at least half the mark's height on every side.
  • Minimum size: mark 16 px or 4 mm; lockup 24 px or 8 mm tall.
  • The wordmark is outlined type. Never retype it next to the mark; use the lockup files.
  • Primary lockup is mint on brand black. On cream or white use the light lockup.
  • Amber is for Labs materials only, never mixed with mint in one composition.
  • Do not rotate, outline, add shadows, change the lean of the microphone, or place the mint mark on mid-tone greens.
  • On photography use a mono version in cream, white or ink.
