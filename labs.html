◆ How we produce

Three roles, one clean pipeline.

Not scraped. Not scripted. Generated live, verified live, packaged automatically.

Step 01 · Record

Live improv, dual-track

Two anonymously-paired players hold a real conversation in the target language, guided by scenario cards. Recorded on separate WebRTC tracks so speakers never bleed into each other.

Live scene · Banking Wahala
Speaker A · frustrated
"I wake up see say POS commot 50k…"
Speaker B · empathetic
"I am so sorry — make I check am."
Step 02 · Verify

Editor polishes live

A third player, a vetted native speaker, corrects slang and Pidgin the STT misheard, tags emotion, timing, and code-switches. Silent auditors sample their work to keep quality honest.

◆ Aligned Corpus

Word-level alignment, human-verified.

Beyond audio and transcripts, our editors align each translated phrase back to the original English prompt, span by span. The result is a corpus that teaches translation models, multilingual embeddings, and low-resource tokenizers how meaning actually maps between languages.

  • Every span in the target language linked to its English source span
  • Handles many-to-one, one-to-many, and null-alignment cases
  • Ground-truth tokenization signal for languages without settled tokenizers
  • Verified by Certified Aligners — a specialist editor tier
  • Ships alongside the standard manifest as `alignments[]` per turn

◆ Lab questions

The things procurement and ML leads ask.

What formats do you deliver? +

Lossless WAV or FLAC (16 kHz or 24 kHz, mono or dual-channel stereo, 16-bit PCM), a per-session manifest.jsonl with full turn metadata, a Parquet index for fast query, a per-speaker consent log, and a commercial-use license. Everything Hugging Face Datasets and PyTorch DataLoader compatible.

How do you handle PII? +

Scenarios are synthetic by design, so speakers are prompted with fake names, accounts and addresses. Any stray real names or numbers are auto-redacted by a named-entity model before storage. PII lives in an isolated, encrypted database physically separated from audio, and audio is indexed only by cryptographic UUIDs. Zero identifiable metrics reach your bucket.

Can we specify custom scenarios or personas? +

Yes — that is our Tier 3 Bespoke offering. You give us the scripts, domain lexicon and target dialects; we deal cards, run scenes, verify, and ship. Exclusivity is available (single-buyer license) at a premium.

How is data delivered to us? +

Three options: (1) presigned S3 or GCS URLs with time-bound expiry and TLS 1.3, (2) direct IAM bucket-to-bucket sync with RBAC and IP whitelisting, (3) AWS Clean Rooms or Snowflake Data Clean Rooms on our Enterprise tier — you fine-tune inside the clean room without raw audio ever leaving. Streaming REST API with OAuth2 is available for training-loop integration.

How fast can we get a pilot corpus? +

For our current live locales (pcm-NG, yo-NG, sw-KE), a 5–10 hour pilot corpus in ~10 business days. Bespoke scenarios add 2 weeks for scripting and onboarding. Aligned Corpus adds ~30% to turnaround time.

What are the usage rights? +

Standard tiers ship with a perpetual, worldwide, commercial-use license (non-exclusive). Exclusivity is available on Bespoke tier. Derivative model training is unrestricted. Redistribution of the raw audio is not.

Do you handle data residency? +

Yes — regional buckets in EU, US, Africa, and Asia. Documented cross-border transfer mechanisms (SCCs / adequacy). A DPIA is available on request for enterprise procurement teams.
