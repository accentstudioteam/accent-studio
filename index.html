06 · Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I actually get paid?
You earn per verified hour of audio, multiplied by your QA tier. Cash out to M-Pesa or Paystack/Flutterwave from $5, Stripe or PayPal from $10, USDC from $2. Payouts land within 7 business days of your tally closing.
Do I need a good microphone or fast internet?
No. A mid range phone in a quiet room is the target setup. Ping-Pong is fully async, so patchy internet is fine. Live Arena needs a stable connection for the session length, about 5 minutes.
Who do I play with?
Strangers, always. Blind Duet pairing is anonymous by design and engineered so people who know each other never match. It keeps scenes fresh and the data honest.
What data is collected about me?
Your voice recordings from scenes, your ratings, and payout details for your chosen rail. Your identity is stored in an isolated encrypted vault, decoupled from your audio, which is indexed only by an anonymous ID. Labs never see who you are.
I'm a lab. How do I license a corpus?
Head to the labs page for schema, sample bundles and delivery formats, or write to us directly. Pilot corpora ship in weeks, not quarters.
