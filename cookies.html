Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30 · Version 1.0

Short version. We use one functional preference (your theme choice) and one privacy-preserving analytics cookie. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site trackers. You can reject non-essential cookies from the banner shown on first visit, or reset your choice at the bottom of this page.

1 · What is a cookie

A cookie is a small text file a website places in your browser. Some cookies are essential for the site to work; others help us understand how the site is used, or remember preferences you've set. We take the minimum-footprint approach.

3 · What we do not use

  • Advertising / marketing cookies
  • Third-party cross-site trackers
  • Social-media pixels (Facebook, TikTok, etc.)
  • Google Analytics or any other cookie-heavy analytics tool that identifies individuals

4 · Your choice

On your first visit we show a cookie banner with two clear options: Accept all or Essential only. Selecting "Essential only" disables analytics; you can still fully use the site.

5 · Reset your choice

If you'd like to change your mind, click below to clear your saved preference. You'll see the banner again on your next visit.
