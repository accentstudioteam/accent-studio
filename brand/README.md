# Accent Studio brand assets

Everything here is generated from one locked geometry. Use the SVGs wherever you can (web, decks, print shops); the PNGs are for places that cannot take vectors.

## The mark
Six-sided body (flat top, two long sides, clipped feet, flat base) with a capsule microphone knocked out as negative space. The mic leans 14° to the right from its foot, the pill sits on the body's centre line, and the neck cuts straight out through the base.

## The lockup rule
The wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold converted to outlines with -0.035em tracking. Its ink height equals the mark's height and its baseline sits on the mark's bottom edge. The gap between mark and wordmark is 0.3× the mark height. Never retype the wordmark in live text next to the mark; use the lockup files.

Lockup box: 858.64 × 86 units. Mark box: 94 × 86 units.

## Folders
- `svg/mark` — the icon alone in mint, amber, ink, cream, spruce, white, black.
- `svg/lockup` — horizontal icon + wordmark. `dark` (mint on the brand black) is primary. `light` (ink + spruce) is for cream and white. `dark-amber` is the Labs page variant. `mono-*` are single-colour versions for embroidery, engraving, one-colour print.
- `svg/stacked` — icon centred above a smaller wordmark, for square placements.
- `svg/wordmark` — the wordmark alone.
- `png/mark` — square transparent PNGs from 16 to 1024 px.
- `png/lockup`, `png/stacked`, `png/wordmark` — transparent PNGs at 256 and 1024 px tall, plus on-background versions.
- `png/app-icon` — mark on the brand black at 180, 192, 512, 1024 px (iOS, Android, stores).
- `png/social` — 1200 × 630 card for link previews.
- `favicon` — `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon.svg`, Apple touch icon, PWA icons and `site.webmanifest`. Copies live at the site root and in `studio-app/public`.
- `print` — vector PDFs for print, plus `Accent_Studio_Logo_Sheet.pdf` showing every variant.

## Rules of use
- Clear space: at least half the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum size: mark 16 px or 4 mm; lockup 24 px or 8 mm tall.
- Do not rotate, outline, add shadows, change the lean, or place the mint mark on mid-tone greens.
- On photography use a mono version in cream or ink.
